# Image credits

The photos in this folder are generic, illustrative pictures of each place type. They do **not** show the venues in the demo dataset, which are fictional, and the app captions every one of them as illustrative ("صورة توضيحية").

All photos come from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The license does not require attribution; the photographers are credited here anyway.
